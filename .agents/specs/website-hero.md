# SPEC — Website Hero

## Purpose

Make a founder understand LangDrift in the first viewport.

## Audience

Founder / Owner / CEO / executive leadership.

## Required hierarchy

1. Product Vision movement question: `Why did your Product Vision fall from 91% to 73%?`
2. Supporting explanation: what changed, who moved it, why, and whether it was intentional.
3. Signature Product Vision / Drift Curve in the first viewport.
4. At least one directly attributable event on the curve.
5. Intentional vs Unexplained meaning visible without engineering terminology.

## States

- light / dark;
- reduced motion;
- selected curve event;
- demo disclosure;
- keyboard focus on curve events.

## Non-goals

- PRD/ADR/Git/CI-led marketing;
- final Product Vision formula;
- invented pricing or entitlements;
- decorative chart without causal meaning.

## Acceptance

- founder can identify the metric, movement, why, who, and classification in one viewport;
- `91 → 73` is explicitly illustrative/demo data;
- hero grid does not render hundreds of DOM tiles;
- semantic state is not communicated by color alone.
