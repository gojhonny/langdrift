# SPEC — Mobile Executive Inquiry

## Purpose

Make the PWA an executive companion rather than a squeezed desktop dashboard.

## Principle

> Glance first. Ask second. Analyze on demand.

## Above the fold

1. LangDrift + selected product.
2. Product Vision current value and recent movement.
3. Intentional / Unexplained summary.
4. Voice Orb.
5. bounded suggested questions.

## Inquiry

Initial Voice stays deterministic over known executive intents, including:

```text
drift_this_week
explain_product_vision
unexplained_changes
```

Unknown questions must not imply open-ended engineering reasoning.

## Evolution detail

The full curve is one tap away. It is not the dominant home element.

## Largest movement

A compact contextual card may show the top event, people, delta, classification, and a path to evolution detail.

## Accessibility / PWA

- tap equivalents for desktop hover;
- comfortable touch targets;
- safe-area handling;
- explicit offline/degraded behavior when introduced;
- do not silently persist sensitive organization data offline;
- valid installable PWA remains required.

## Acceptance

- Product Vision and Orb are adjacent above the fold;
- conversation begins early;
- only three primary prompts are shown initially;
- complete curve is available on demand;
- demo values remain disclosed.
