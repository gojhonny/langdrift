# SPEC — Dashboard Executive IA

## Purpose

Let leadership answer: where are we now, why did Product Vision move, who was involved, and what needs attention?

## Primary navigation

```text
Overview
Evolution
Decisions
People
Reports

Settings
```

`Ask LangDrift` is a persistent action rather than a navigation destination.

## Overview

- Product Vision is dominant.
- Signature curve follows immediately.
- `Why did it move?` decomposition is visible.
- Intentional / Unexplained explain movement rather than compete as equal KPIs.
- attention items are explicit.
- evidence coverage is contextual trust information, not a default executive KPI.

## Evolution

- curve;
- timeline/events;
- auditable baseline;
- classification filters: All / Intentional / Unexplained / Under Review;
- grouping: Event / Product Area / Team.

## Decisions

Executive summary first, then why, who, impact, timeline, and evidence disclosure.

## People

Show participation, ownership, decisions, implementations, approvals, and reviews. Do not create employee performance scoring.

## Reports

Weekly/monthly/decision-oriented executive summaries over the same product truth.

## Accessibility

- keyboard-focusable curve events;
- visible focus;
- text equivalents;
- semantic labels in addition to color;
- reduced motion.

## Acceptance

- approximately five primary analytical destinations;
- Evidence is contextual, not primary navigation;
- Product Area and Team behave as dimensions;
- Intentional/Unexplained behave as classifications;
- Product Vision never appears as an unexplained AI probability.
